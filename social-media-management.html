Module Overview

This module is designed to turn learners into professional social media managers capable of growing brands, engaging audiences, and delivering measurable business results. Whether you're managing your own brand or handling clients' accounts, this course covers everything from content creation to advertising and analytics.

Learners will gain the skills to strategically plan, execute, and optimize social media campaigns that drive engagement, traffic, and revenue, preparing them to earn in dollars on freelance platforms or full-time remote positions.
